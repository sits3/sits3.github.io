## Heel Raise Measurement Device for Plantarflexion Physiotherapy 

### Team Member
* A/P Indriyati Atmosukarto
* A/P Jeannie Lee
* Asst Prof Goh Weihan
* ICT SE student helpers 2017 and UoG CS FYP
* Industry Partner: Kwong Wai Shiu Hospital (KWSH)

### Project Start & End Date
* Nov 2015 – August  2017

### Channel of Collaboration and/or Funding
* UoG CS FYP Project
* SIT Ignition Grant

### Problem to Solve
* Subjective measurement of physiotherapy exercises

### Solution and Notable Contribution
* A flexible cloud based infrastructure framework was developed to support remote data gathering and analysis 
* Mobile app to accompany newly developed device to provide objective measurements

### Notable Outcome(s) / Progress and Next Step / Potential of Wider Application
* Best Poster Award “Heel Raise Measurement Device for Plantarflexion Physiotherapy”, CHALK event (Central Health Action & Learning Kampong), 2018
•	Project finalist “Heel Raise Measurement” of the 5th Asia Pacific Eldercare Innovation Awards (2017)
