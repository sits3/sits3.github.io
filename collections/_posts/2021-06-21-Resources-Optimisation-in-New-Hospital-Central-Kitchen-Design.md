### Channel of Collaboration and/or Funding
* Integrated Work Study Programme (IWSP)

### Problem to Solve
* In the new Woodlands Hospital, how should the workflow of the central kitchen be designed so as to maximize its efficiency.

### Solution and Notable Contribution
* Developed a simulation model to study the impact of reducing unnecessary activities and operators’ movement in central kitchen workflow to meet design guidelines and service levels recommended by MOH.

### Publication
* Kian Ann Chan, Mack Jia Jia Pan, Beng Tee Chua, Xiu Ming Hu and Malcolm Yoke Hean Low, “[Resources Optimisation in New Hospital Central Kitchen Design – A Discrete Event Simulation Approach](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-15-1078-6_22)”, In Proceedings of the 19th Asia Simulation Conference (AsiaSim 2019), pp. 251-259, 30 October – 1 November 2019, Singapore.
