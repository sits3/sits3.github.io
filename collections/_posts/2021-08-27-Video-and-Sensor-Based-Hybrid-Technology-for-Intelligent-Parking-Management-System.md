*	A/P Indriyati Atmosukarto (ICT)

### Project Start & End Date
* 2016 - 2018

### Channel of Collaboration and/or Funding
* SIT Ignition Grant

### Problem to Solve
* Too much time is taken for drivers to find empty parking slot 

### Solution and Notable Contribution
* Develop and intelligent parking management system based on hybrid sensor approach with both video and sensor-based subsystems
* Video analytics solution provide zone level, sensor solution provide lot level occupancy tracking

### Notable Outcome(s) / Progress and Next Step / Potential of Wider Application
* Deployed pilot at SIT@Dover parking lot
